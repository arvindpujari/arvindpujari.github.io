Arvind Pujari

I am currently a Tenure-Track Assistant Professor at the Institute for Materials Research at Tohoku University in Sendai, Japan. I lead an independent research group focusing on developing scalable operando tools for battery research. My position is part of the $2.5 billion UREX Grant designed to make Tohoku University Japan's first university of international excellence.

I have a PhD in Physics from the Cavendish Laboratory at the University of Cambridge (2020–24). My PhD focused on interactions between light and batteries for enhanced charging rates and operando spectroscopy. I then joined Illumion Ltd. (2025–26), a battery instrumentation startup based in Cambridge, as an R&D scientist. There, I worked with both academic and industrial customers to develop tools to accelerate battery research.

Before this, I completed my undergraduate degree in Metallurgical and Materials Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IIT Madras). There, I was awarded the Dr. Shankar Dayal Sharma Gold Medal as the undergraduate student with the best all-round performance in curricular and extracurricular activities during 2016–2020.

Find out more at my group website: batlab.jp.

Research

My research focuses on using interactions between light and batteries to enhance their energy storage and study electrochemical mechanisms. I am particularly interested in developing scalable optical operando techniques to study batteries in real time.
